# SafaQrcode Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-19

SafaQrcode is a Safari extension for iOS and macOS that generates a QR code for the webpage you are currently viewing.

## We collect no personal data

SafaQrcode does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

## How the extension works

- When you open the SafaQrcode extension, it reads the URL of the currently active tab. This happens **only when you actively open the extension** (via the Safari extension permission "activeTab").
- That URL is used **only** to generate a QR code on your device. It is never sent to any server, never stored, and never shared.
- Copying the URL and downloading the QR code image are also handled entirely on your device.

## Data storage

SafaQrcode stores no data on your device and uses no network requests. Everything runs locally.

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: `zhihaofans@hotmail.com`.

# SafaQrcode 隐私政策

最近更新：2026-08-19

SafaQrcode 是一款适用于 iOS 和 macOS 的 Safari 扩展，用于为当前网页生成二维码。

## 我们不收集任何个人数据

SafaQrcode **不收集、不存储、不传输、不分享**任何个人数据。没有账号、没有统计、没有广告、没有追踪，也没有任何第三方 SDK。

## 扩展的工作原理

- 当你打开 SafaQrcode 扩展时，它会读取当前活动标签页的网址。这**仅在你主动打开扩展时**发生（通过 Safari 的「activeTab」权限）。
- 该网址**仅用于**在你的设备上生成二维码。它绝不会发送到任何服务器、不会存储、也不会分享。
- 复制网址、下载二维码图片也完全在你的设备上完成。

## 数据存储

SafaQrcode 不在此设备上存储任何数据，也不发起任何网络请求。所有操作都在本地完成。
