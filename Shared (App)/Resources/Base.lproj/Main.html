First-Time Setup Guide

  1. Open any webpage in Safari.
  2. Click the Extensions (puzzle) icon in the Safari toolbar and choose SafaQrcode.
  3. Enable SafaQrcode in Settings → Safari → Extensions, then tap the size icon at the left of the address bar and choose SafaQrcode.
  4. The extension popup shows a QR code for the current page.
  5. Scan it with your phone camera to open the page on your phone; you can also tap “Copy URL” or “Download QR Code”.
